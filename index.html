What I deliberately did not do

No countdown timer on the offer paywall. The pattern only fires when a real discount exists to count down to. Fishbrain publishes no discounted SKU, so a timer here would be manufactured urgency — it erodes trust in every category and is the one change on this list that can cost more than it earns.

No invented "Pro members catch X% more" on the paywall hero. Fishbrain's help centre does carry a 60%-more-fish claim, but I could not retrieve it verbatim from a first-party page (Cloudflare blocks the support host). It is left off the screens and flagged in the source table rather than shipped on a guess.

No fabricated projection on the payoff screen. The summary echoes back exactly what the user selected plus counts Fishbrain already publishes. It never predicts a catch, a weight or a date — that would be the broken promise rebuilt with better typography.

No question cut. All three of the app's existing questions survive verbatim, with their original controls. See the note below on the one option I would test removing.

What I need from you to ship this

1. The four paywall carousel photos. The hero scenes here are drawn stand-ins. The archetype is hero-led because your reference is hero-led — it needs your actual photography, not my illustration.

2. The species illustrations. Same story: the grid control, spacing and check badge are yours; the fish art is placeholder.

3. Confirmation that the 3-day trial is live on the Annual SKU in every storefront you'd run this in. The whole offer paywall rests on it.

4. Whether the Quarter Power Fishing Package ($41.99) is still purchasable — it's in the App Store IAP list but not on the marketing site.

5. The 60%-more-fish claim's source and current wording, if you want it on the hero. It's your strongest single line and it is currently on none of your paywall frames.

6. Whether the app really filters the species grid and map by the water type answer — the new branch assumes it does, because the App Store listing says species-targeted search ships.

The one thing I'd test cutting — and didn't

Option 7 on question 1, "Other / Still deciding", is the only element in the whole flow that collects nothing usable: it cannot route a branch, cannot fill a chip on the payoff screen, and cannot personalise the paywall headline. Everything else you ask earns its place. You said keep every question, so it stayed — and to keep it honest rather than inert, selecting it now changes what the payoff screen says instead of leaving a blank: the summary switches from a species-led line to a water-led one. That's the rule this build follows throughout: if an answer stays in, it has to show up downstream.

Two places every kept answer surfaces: the loader (it names the water and the species while it works) and the payoff screen (it prints the interests as chips, the water as a map thumbnail, and the species as the headline). The paywall headline then names the species and the water a third time. Toggle any answer on the chips below the phone and all three change.
